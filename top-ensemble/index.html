Top
Ensemble

Spin a top across the table and it conducts the band. Every patch of table is a different musician, so wherever your top wanders, the music follows. Now and then a soloist steps up and plays a note for every step of its path.

  1. Prop your phone or laptop so the camera looks down at the table and doesn't move.
  2. Start with the table empty for a second, so it can learn what the table looks like.
  3. Spin a top and let it drift. If it gets lost, tap the top on screen.
